Privacy Policy — SyllabAI

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

SyllabAI ("we," "our," or "the Service") is a student syllabus and academic calendar organizer. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and access, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have when using SyllabAI at syllabai.tech.

1. Information We Collect and Access

  • Google Account & Profile Information: When you choose to sign in with Google, we access your basic Google profile details (name and email address) solely to display your active session inside SyllabAI.
  • Google Calendar Data: When you authorize Google Calendar access and explicitly click to sync events, SyllabAI accesses your calendar list to locate or create course-specific calendars (e.g., named after your course code) and create calendar events for your class assignments, exams, and deadlines.
  • Syllabus & Course Schedule Data: Course details you enter (course code, course name, meeting days/times, and calendar color), syllabus files or text you upload for scanning, and extracted or manually created deadlines.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • In-Browser Syllabus Extraction: Uploaded syllabus files (such as PDF, TXT, MD, CSV, or HTML files) are parsed directly inside your web browser to extract plain text and detect assignment or exam dates for your review.
  • User-Initiated Calendar Sync & Export: SyllabAI only sends events to your Google Calendar or downloads an .ics file when you explicitly click the sync or export buttons.
  • No Advertising or Data Sales: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Google user data to third parties, and we never use your data for targeted advertising.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

SyllabAI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only request and use the minimum Google OAuth permissions necessary to sign you in and sync the course schedules you initiate.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google Calendar data or uploaded syllabus content unless required for security investigation, legal compliance, or with your explicit consent.

4. Data Storage, Retention & Your Controls

  • Session-Based Processing: Uploaded syllabus text, course entries, and OAuth access tokens are held only in your active browser session memory and are cleared when you sign out or close/refresh the page.
  • Revoking Google Access: You can revoke SyllabAI's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.
  • Deleting Calendar Data: You can delete individual items inside SyllabAI before syncing, or delete any synced course calendars and events directly in Google Calendar.

5. Third-Party Services

SyllabAI interacts with Google APIs (Google Identity Services and Google Calendar API) when you choose to sign in with Google, and is served via static web hosting. Your use of Google services is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
